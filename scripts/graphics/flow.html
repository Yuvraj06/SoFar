How SoFar keeps you spoiler free

Every question goes through the same steps, in this order.

1

You press send

SoFar reads your playback time at that exact moment.

→
2

Cut at your time

Only subtitle lines you have already seen are kept. Future lines never leave the extension.

→
3

Extra facts

Cast from TMDB and a filtered web search for real world facts and background. Plot is stripped out.

→
4

AI drafts an answer

Gemini or Groq, told to use only what has been shown so far.

→
5

Spoiler check

A second AI call reviews the draft against what you have seen.

SafeThe answer is shown.
UnsafeOne retry, then "We don't know that yet."
